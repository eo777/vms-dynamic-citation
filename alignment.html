Coverage and limits

The conservative spatial policy excludes shared or disputed word assignments, cross-locus merges, disconnected pieces and unresolved layout cases. A spelling difference alone is not a rejection. Long gallows and large original rectangles are retained. These checks are conservative screening, not a claim that every accepted word has been visually verified.

The image overlay uses Voynichese's own cropped display image and its exact display rectangles. Original XML pixel coordinates are retained separately for analyses. No proportional overlay on an independently downloaded Yale scan is treated as registered. No original XML rectangle or ZL spelling was changed.

Previously saved copying reconstructions use the earlier alignment. Their scores need re-analysis; the current live source cones use the reviewed word-level geometry.
